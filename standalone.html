01 — Heat · How does heat move?

      
Energy arrives at the equator and leaves at the poles.

      
Sunlight is unevenly distributed, so the atmosphere works permanently to redistribute it. Raise the mean anomaly and the circulation accelerates: the transport does not stop, it intensifies.

02 — Water · Where does water go?

      
A closed loop with no beginning.

      
Every parcel of water traverses the same circuit. Isolate a stage to follow it through the field; the particles you see are carrying that phase.

      

      

      
Loop residence—

      
Warming increases atmospheric water capacity by roughly 7% per °C — the loop runs faster and heavier.

    

  


  
  

    

      
03 — Cities · Why are cities hotter?

      
Surfaces decide temperature.

      
A city is a thermal object. Concrete stores heat, vegetation evaporates it, water buffers it. Change the composition and watch the field respond.

The same terrain, pushed.
